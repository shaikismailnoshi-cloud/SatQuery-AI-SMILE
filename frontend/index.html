    SatQuery AI — Platform Documentation & User Guide

    Complete guide to agentic multimodal remote sensing intelligence

    🌐
    SatQuery AI System Overview:

    SatQuery AI is a comprehensive multimodal remote-sensing intelligence platform. Building detection is one of its specialized capabilities alongside scene description, land-cover identification, change detection, and optical-SAR radar fusion.

    📖 10-Step Workflow Guide

    1. Upload Satellite Imagery: Drag & drop GeoTIFF, TIFF, PNG, or JPEG imagery.
    2. Select Analysis Mode: Choose between Describe Scene, Locate Buildings, Bi-Temporal Change, or Optical-SAR Fusion.
    3. Ask a Question: Type your query or click the 🎤 mic icon to speak in English or Indian regional languages.
    4. Execute Analysis: Click "Analyze Imagery →" to trigger PyTorch grounded inference.
    5. View Overlays: Inspect detected bounding boxes, change maps, or fusion layers on the central viewer.
    6. Review Summary: Dynamic mode-specific results appear automatically in the result panel.
    7. Use Viewer Controls: Mouse wheel to zoom, drag to pan, or toggle overlays (`🥞`).
    8. Inspect Findings: Click any object or building card to zoom and highlight it on the canvas.
    9. Check Confidence & Limits: Review calibrated model confidence and resolution boundaries.
    10. Export Results: Download JSON metadata, CSV detection tables, or PDF print reports.

    🎯 Four Core Remote Sensing Capabilities

    🌐 Describe Scene

    Analyzes land cover distribution (vegetation, water, urban impervious surfaces, bare soil), terrain composition, and spatial scene context.

    🏢 Locate Buildings

    Runs PyTorch `FasterRCNN_MobileNet_V3_Large_FPN` with multi-scale overlapping tiling to detect and localize residential and commercial roof structures with exact bounding box coordinates.

    ⏰ Bi-Temporal Change

    Compares co-registered satellite images across time (T1 vs T2) to generate pixel-level land-cover alteration maps and calculate percentage change area.

    🎯 Optical-SAR Fusion

    Combines optical spectral reflection with Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) backscatter to penetrate cloud cover and confirm physical structures under adverse atmospheric conditions.
